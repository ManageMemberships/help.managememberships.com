# Janus

**Janus** is an AI assistant inside ManageMemberships, labeled **Beta**. Ask it a question in plain words and it answers: why a member can't get through a door, how a member's billing looks, or how a feature works.

:::warning Janus can make mistakes
Janus is in beta. Check anything important before you act on it.
:::

---

## Finding Janus

Open your profile menu and click **Janus (Beta)**.

Only **owners** and **managers** can use Janus. Staff and accountants don't see it. If you are an owner or manager and don't see **Janus (Beta)** in your profile menu, Janus isn't available for your gym yet.

---

## Asking a Question

Type in the box that says **Ask Janus about your gym…** and click **SEND**, or press **Enter**. Press **Shift+Enter** to start a new line without sending.

Click **New chat** to start a fresh conversation.

---

## Two Ways to Use Janus

### Help pages only (the default)

Until you turn on your gym's data, Janus answers how-to questions from these help pages and from click-through demos. It **cannot see your members, billing or doors.**

### With your gym's data

With your gym's data on, Janus can look up members, billing, door access, classes and how your [Squeeze Pages](./communication/squeeze-pages.md) are doing **for your gym only**.

1. Open Janus and click **Use my gym's data too**.
2. Read the explanation.
3. Tick the box.
4. Click **TURN ON JANUS**.

This applies to your **whole gym**, not just you. You can turn it off any time by clicking **Turn off**.

---

## Point Values

With your gym's data on, Janus can read your point values:

- your active [Point Award Reason Presets](./membership/points-leaderboard.md#point-award-reason-presets) (**Settings → Point Award Reason Presets**)
- the points members get automatically for a check-in, a class attended and their first class booking (**Settings → Portal Settings → Points**)

So you can ask things like *How many points for competing?* or *What are our point presets?*

When you ask Janus to give members points, it uses the matching preset's number. The award still waits on the **Approvals** page until you or a manager approves it. Janus never makes up a number: if nothing matches, it asks you.

**Which number Janus uses:** a number you type in the chat wins, then a matching preset, then your [notes](#what-janus-should-know-about-your-gym). If none of those, Janus asks.

Janus can't change your presets or point settings. It can give each member 1 to 100 points in one request. To give more, or to take points away, use **Award Points** on the member's page.

---

## What Janus Should Know About Your Gym

Type facts about your gym once, like opening hours, house rules or point values, and Janus uses them in every chat.

1. Open Janus and click **What Janus should know about your gym**.
2. Type your notes, up to 2,000 characters.
3. Click **SAVE**. You'll see **Saved.**, and **(saved)** appears next to the heading.

To change your notes, edit them and click **SAVE** again. To remove them, empty the box and click **SAVE**.

Only owners and managers can edit the notes. The box only shows while your gym's data is on. If you click **Turn off**, your notes are kept but not used until you turn Janus back on.

---

## Changes Need Your Approval

Janus never changes anything by itself. It can only **prepare a request**, for example a refund, activating a member or awarding points.

Each request waits on the **Approvals** page, which you open with the **Approvals** button in Janus. An owner or manager looks at each one and clicks **Approve** or **Reject**.

**Nothing changes until someone approves it.**

---

## Privacy

Janus uses a third-party AI. Before anything is sent, names are replaced with anonymous IDs and emails and phone numbers are removed. The link between those IDs and your members stays on our servers.

- Member ids and IP addresses are removed too.
- You still see real names in Janus's answers.
- The AI provider is outside the United States. The box you tick to turn on your gym's data says so.
- Janus can't look up prospects or family members on someone's account. Their names are removed before anything is sent, and Janus will point you to the **Prospects CRM** page or the main member's account instead.
- Your notes from **What Janus should know about your gym** are sent with each message. Before they're sent, your members' names are replaced with anonymous IDs, prospect and family names are removed, and emails and phone numbers are removed. Member names aren't needed in your notes.

:::tip Keep personal details out of your questions and notes
Janus may not recognize a misspelled name or a nickname, or the name of someone who isn't in your gym's records, and it can't recognize a street address. Avoid typing personal details like addresses into the chat or your notes.
:::

## Example Questions

- *How do I set up a class pack?* (works with help pages only)
- *Why can Jane Smith not get through the front door?*
- *How does Sam Lee's billing look?*
- *How many people signed up through my Free Trial squeeze page this month?* (Janus gives you the numbers, not the names. To see the people, use **View submissions** on the Squeeze Pages screen.)
- *How many points for competing?*
- *What are our point presets?*
