# Janus

**Janus** is an AI assistant inside ManageMemberships, labeled **Beta**. Ask it a question in plain words and it answers: why a member can't get through a door, how a member's billing looks, or how a feature works.

:::warning Janus can make mistakes
Janus is in beta. Check anything important before you act on it.
:::

---

## Finding Janus

Open your profile menu and click **Janus (Beta)**.

Only **owners** and **managers** can use Janus. Staff and accountants don't see it. If you are an owner or manager and don't see **Janus (Beta)** in your profile menu, Janus isn't available for your gym yet.

---

## Asking a Question

Type in the box that says **Ask Janus about your gym…** and click **SEND**, or press **Enter**. Press **Shift+Enter** to start a new line without sending.

Click **New chat** to start a fresh conversation.

---

## Two Ways to Use Janus

### Help pages only (the default)

Until you turn on your gym's data, Janus answers how-to questions from these help pages and from click-through demos. It **cannot see your members, billing or doors.**

### With your gym's data

With your gym's data on, Janus can look up members, billing, door access and classes **for your gym only**.

1. Open Janus and click **Use my gym's data too**.
2. Read the explanation.
3. Tick the box.
4. Click **TURN ON JANUS**.

This applies to your **whole gym**, not just you. You can turn it off any time by clicking **Turn off**.

---

## Changes Need Your Approval

Janus never changes anything by itself. It can only **prepare a request**, for example a refund, activating a member or awarding points.

Each request waits on the **Approvals** page, which you open with the **Approvals** button in Janus. An owner or manager looks at each one and clicks **Approve** or **Reject**.

**Nothing changes until someone approves it.**

---

## Privacy

Janus uses a third-party AI. Before anything is sent, names are replaced with anonymous IDs and emails and phone numbers are removed. The link between those IDs and your members stays on our servers.

- Member ids and IP addresses are removed too.
- You still see real names in Janus's answers.
- The AI provider is outside the United States. The box you tick to turn on your gym's data says so.
- Janus can't look up prospects or family members on someone's account. Their names are removed before anything is sent, and Janus will point you to the **Prospects CRM** page or the main member's account instead.

:::tip Keep personal details out of your questions
Janus may not recognize a misspelled name or a nickname, and it can't recognize a street address. Avoid typing personal details like addresses into the chat.
:::
