# Clients and Assigning Programs

Your **clients** are the people you coach: usually members, but staff can be clients too. You flag them as clients, then assign them a program. A client can have one current program at a time.

:::note Beta
Training is marked **Beta** in the app, so things may change.
:::

---

## The Clients Tab

Go to **Add-ons → Training → Clients**.

- **Trainers** see their own clients.
- **Owners and managers** see every coach's clients, with **Coach:** under each name.

Use the chips to filter: **All**, **On a program**, **No program** and **Needs attention**. Each chip shows how many clients it holds. You can also **Search clients** by name or email, or pick a membership level.

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Name and email. Amber pills give the reasons a client [needs attention](follow-up.md#needs-attention). |
| **Current program** | The program, with a **Personal plan** badge if it's [personal](client-page.md#edit-plan-personal-plans). If the client has no current program: **No program**, and the ended program if there was one. |
| **Progress** | *Starts Mon 26 Oct*, *Week 2 of 4* or *Finished* |
| **Last workout** | *today*, *yesterday* or a date |

Click **Member** to sort by name. You can show 25, 50 or 100 clients per page.

Each row has:

- **Assign program**, or **Change program** if the client already has one
- **View**, which opens the [client's page](client-page.md). It appears once the client has had a program.
- **⋯ → Unflag**

## Flagging Clients

1. Click **Flag clients**.
2. Owners and managers choose the **Coach**. A trainer always flags clients for themselves, so they don't see this.
3. Under **Members**, search your active members and staff by name or email, and pick one or more. Anyone who is already that coach's client shows **Already a client**.
4. Click **Flag clients**.

A member can be a client of more than one coach.

Owners, managers, staff and accountants can be clients too, for example a coach's colleague or an owner trying out a program. A staff client can be assigned a program and uses **My Training** and **My progress** in the member portal, like a member. Staff clients don't get the program email, comment emails or push notifications.

### Unflagging

Click **⋯ → Unflag** and confirm.

- If the member has other coaches, they stay with those coaches and their program stays the same.
- If this was their last coach, their current program ends too. Its history is kept.

## Assigning a Program

You can assign a program to one client or to many at once. Open **Assign program** from any of these places:

- the **Clients** tab: the **Assign program** button, after ticking clients, or on a client's row
- **Assign** on the **Programs** tab
- **Assign to more members** in the program builder
- **Change program** on a client's page

Then:

1. Under **Who**, tick the clients. Each one shows one of these:
   - **No program**
   - **On \{program\}**
   - **On personal plan · \{program\}**
   - **On this program**, which can't be ticked

   Inactive members show **Inactive** and are skipped.
2. Choose the **Program**.
3. Choose the **Start date**. Programs start on the **Monday** of the week you pick. For example, any day from Mon 2 Nov to Sun 8 Nov starts the program on Mon 2 Nov.
4. If anyone you ticked is already on a program, choose whether to tick **Replace their current program**:
   - **Not ticked:** those members are skipped.
   - **Ticked:** their current program ends today and the new one starts. The old program's history is kept. A personal plan ends too.
5. Check the summary, for example *Assign Strength Block to 12 members starting Mon 16 Nov.* Then click **Assign to N members**.

You can assign up to 100 members at a time.

### What the member gets

- An email, **Your new program: \{program\}**, with an **Open My Training** button.
- On Core plans and above, a push notification as well.
- A **Training** link in their member portal.

See [For Members](for-members.md).
