# A Client's Training Page

Each client has a page with their program, their logs and their progress. You can open it in any of these ways:

- click **View** on the **Clients** tab
- click a workout on **Recent workouts**
- click **Open** on **Needs attention**

Trainers can open their own clients' pages. Owners and managers can open anyone's.

:::note Beta
Training is marked **Beta** in the app, so things may change.
:::

---

## The Header

The header shows:

- the client's name and membership level
- their program, marked **Personal plan** if it's a personal one
- the current week, for example *Week 2 of 4*
- their last workout

It has two buttons:

- **Edit plan**, to change this client's plan only (see [below](#edit-plan-personal-plans))
- **Change program**, or **Assign program** if they have none

The tabs are **Logs**, **Progress**, **Body weight & photos** and **Maxes**.

---

## Logs

The left side lists the program **week by week**, newest first. The current week is marked **Now**. A week can show:

- a green check when every workout was logged
- an amber number for missed or partly logged workouts
- **Opened early**
- an opening date, such as *Opens 23 Nov*, for a week that isn't open yet

Each day shows **Logged**, a count such as *2 of 5*, **Today**, **Upcoming** or **Not logged**. Click a day to see that workout.

### The workout

The workout card shows:

- chips such as **All 5 logged**, **1 PR**, **Felt: Hard** and **Readiness 3.8**
- each exercise's **Planned** sets next to what the member logged in **Set 1**, **Set 2** and so on, for example *135 lb × 8 · RPE 9*
- reps in amber when they are below that set's plan
- a **PR** badge on a personal record
- the **Member note**, if they wrote one
- the comments and **Thumbs-up** for this workout. See [Comments and Thumbs-up](follow-up.md#comments-and-thumbs-up).

On a wide screen you also see **Best weight** (with a chart), **Personal records** and **Body weight** cards.

### Open week now

Each week opens for members on its Monday. To let a client start the next week early, click **Open week N now**, then **Open week N**. They can see and log it straight away. They aren't notified, and only the next locked week can be opened.

### Ended programs

An ended program opens read-only, and its comments are closed. If exercises were removed from the program after the client logged them, those logs are listed under **Logged for exercises no longer in the program**.

---

## Progress

The **Progress** tab shows every exercise the client has logged, across all their programs, with their personal records and best set. Click an exercise to see a chart and its full history (**Date**, **Program**, **Sets**, **Best**).

If readiness check-ins are on, a **Readiness** chart shows each workout's score. The dashed line is your low-readiness threshold.

A **personal record (PR)** is a set that beats every earlier day in one of these ways:

- a heavier weight for the same number of reps
- the heaviest weight yet
- for exercises without weight, more reps

The first day an exercise is logged is never a PR.

---

## Body Weight

The **Body weight & photos** tab shows a chart and every weigh-in, with who added it.

To add a weigh-in:

1. Fill in the **Date** (up to today), the **Weight** and the **Unit** (lb or kg).
2. Click **Save weigh-in**.

If there's already a weigh-in on that date, it's replaced. Use **Edit** or **Delete** on a weigh-in to change or remove it.

Weights can be 44 to 882 lb (20 to 400 kg). Members can also add their own weight on My Training.

## Edit Plan (Personal Plans)

Normally everyone on a program follows the same plan, and changing the program changes it for all of them. To change the plan for **one client only**:

1. On their page, click **Edit plan**.
2. When asked **Edit \{name\}'s plan?**, click **Edit plan**. This makes a **personal copy** of their program, and their logs move onto it.
3. The program builder opens for their plan. Change their weeks, days, exercises and sets, then click **Save plan**.

What to know about personal plans:

- The original program (the **template**) and everyone else on it don't change. Later changes to the template don't reach this client.
- The client sees no difference. The plan keeps the program's name and description.
- You can't change the name, length or description of a personal plan. **Copy week** changes only this client's plan.
- The **Personal plan** card shows which weeks differ from the template.
- **Reset week N to template** puts a week back to the template's version. It shows **Already started** once the client has logged, checked in, written a note or commented in that week.
- Personal plans don't appear on the **Programs** tab. If you assign a new program with **Replace their current program** ticked, the personal plan ends and its history is kept.

To keep editing the same plan later, click **Edit plan** again.
