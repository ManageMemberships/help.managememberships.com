# Recent Workouts, Comments and Needs Attention

Training helps you keep up with your clients:

- **Recent workouts** shows what they finished.
- **Comments and a thumbs-up** let you answer them.
- **Needs attention** lists the clients who need a look.

Trainers see their own clients. Owners and managers see every coach's clients.

:::note Beta
Training is marked **Beta** in the app, so things may change.
:::

---

## Recent Workouts

Go to **Add-ons → Training → Recent workouts**. It lists workouts your clients **finished** or **commented on**, newest first. A workout is **finished** when every exercise of the day is logged.

Each row shows the client, the workout and program, the **Week · day**, and when it was finished (or *Not finished yet*). These badges can appear:

| Badge | Means |
|---|---|
| **New reply** | The member wrote since your last visit |
| **New** | The workout was finished since your last visit |
| **1 PR** | The number of personal records in this workout |
| **Readiness 3.8** | The readiness score, with **· Low** when it's low |
| **Felt: Hard** | How the member rated the workout |
| **Program ended** | The program has ended since |

Click a row to open the client's page at that workout. Use **Search clients** to find a client by name.

A **red dot** on the tab means something new. Opening the tab clears the dot. The **New** badges show during that visit and are gone the next time you open the tab.

## Comments and Thumbs-up

Open a workout on the client's **Logs** tab.

- Type in **Comment on this workout…** and click **Post**.
- Click **Thumbs-up**. It changes to **Thumbs-up given**. Click it again to take it back.
- You can **Edit** your own comments. Anyone can **Delete** their own comments, and owners and managers can delete any comment.

The member sees your comments and thumbs-up on My Training and can reply in the same thread. Once a program has ended, its comments are closed.

### Who is told

| When | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| A coach comments | The member | An email with the comment. On Core plans and above, also a push notification. |
| A coach gives the first thumbs-up | The member | A push notification, on Core plans and above |
| A member replies | Their coaches who are owners or managers | A push notification, on Core plans and above |
| A member finishes a workout | Their coaches who are owners or managers | A push notification, on Core plans and above |

Trainers see new activity from the red dot on **Recent workouts**. Editing a comment, or giving a thumbs-up again, doesn't notify anyone.

## Needs Attention

Go to **Add-ons → Training → Needs attention**. It lists the clients who need following up, with the reasons why. The tab shows how many clients are listed, and the **Needs attention** chip on the Clients tab filters to the same clients.

The list is worked out from what members log. **A reason goes away by itself** once the member logs or a coach answers.

| Reason | Shows when | Goes away when |
|---|---|---|
| **Missed workout** | A planned workout in the look-back period has nothing logged. Today doesn't count. | The member logs it, or it's older than the look-back |
| **Partly logged** | Half or fewer of a workout's exercises were logged, or fewer sets than planned | It's fully logged, or it's older than the look-back |
| **Unanswered reply** | The member wrote and no coach has commented since. A thumbs-up doesn't count. | A coach comments |
| **Low readiness** | The member's latest check-in is at or below your low-readiness setting | A newer check-in isn't low |
| **Hard workout** (off by default) | A workout was rated Very hard, or its average felt RPE is 9.5 or more | It's older than the look-back |
| **No workout in N days** | Nothing has been logged for that many days since the program started | The member logs a workout |
| **Final week or finished** | The program is in its last week, or has finished | A new program is assigned |
| **No program** | The client has no current program | A program is assigned |

Click a reason chip to filter the list. Each client has:

- **Open**, which goes to their page at the most relevant workout
- **Assign program**, when their program has finished or they have none

### Needs attention settings

Owners and managers set these on **Settings → Needs attention**:

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Look back (days)** | How many days back to check for missed, partly logged and hard workouts, low readiness and unanswered replies (1 to 28) | 7 |
| **No workout after (days)** | How many days without a workout before **No workout** shows (1 to 60) | 7 |
| **Hard workout** | Shows workouts rated Very hard, or with an average felt RPE of 9.5 or more | Off |
| **Unanswered reply** | Shows members who wrote and have had no coach comment since | On |

Low readiness uses the threshold set in **Readiness check-in**. Click **Save needs attention**.
