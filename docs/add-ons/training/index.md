# Training

**Training** is an add-on for gyms that coach members. Coaches build workout **programs**, pick the members who are their **clients**, and assign each client a program. Members log every set they do from your member portal, and coaches follow their progress, comment on workouts and see who needs a look.

:::note Beta
Training is marked **Beta** in the app, so things may change.
:::

Training is **off by default**. An owner or manager turns it on from the [Marketplace](../../settings/marketplace.md).

---

## Turning It On

1. Go to **Settings → Marketplace**.
2. On the **Training** card, click **Enable**.
3. In the **Turn Training on for your gym?** box, click **Turn on**.

An **Add-ons** menu now appears after **Calendar**, with a **Training** link.

To turn it off, click **Disable**, then **Turn off**. Nothing is deleted: programs, clients and logs come back when you turn Training on again.

---

## Who Can Do What

Coaches are:

- **Owners**
- **Managers**
- **Staff** who have the **Trainer/Available for Classes** checkbox ticked on the [Staff](../../settings/staff.md) page

Only coaches see **Training** in the **Add-ons** menu.

:::note
The **Trainer/Available for Classes** checkbox isn't shown on Free and Basic (Starter) plans, so you can't mark new trainers there. Staff who were already marked as trainers, for example before a plan change, can still coach.
:::

| Task | Owners and managers | Trainers | Members |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turn Training on or off | Yes | No | No |
| Create, edit, duplicate and delete programs | Yes, any program | Yes, any program | No |
| Add exercises and demo videos | Yes | Yes | No |
| See clients | Every coach's clients | Their own clients | No |
| Flag clients | For any coach | For themselves | No |
| Assign programs, edit a client's plan, open a week early, save maxes and body weight, comment | Any client | Their own clients | No |
| See and handle performance flags | Every coach's clients | Their own clients | No |
| Delete someone else's comment | Yes | No | No |
| Change Training **Settings** | Yes | No (they can view them) | No |
| Log their own workouts on **My Training** | — | — | Yes |

## The Training Pages

Go to **Add-ons → Training**. The tabs are:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Programs** | Your gym's programs. See [Programs and the Program Builder](programs.md). |
| **Clients** | Flag clients and assign programs. See [Clients and Assigning Programs](clients.md). |
| **Recent workouts** | Workouts your clients finished or commented on. A red dot means something new. See [Recent Workouts, Comments and Needs Attention](follow-up.md). |
| **Needs attention** | Clients who need following up, with a count. Same article. |
| **Flags** | Sets that clearly beat their plan, so a training max may be out of date, with a count. See [Performance Flags and Flag Rules](performance-flags.md). |
| **Exercises** | Your exercise library and demo videos. See [Exercise Library and Demo Videos](exercises.md). |
| **Settings** | Weight ranges, readiness check-in, Needs attention, flag rules and lifts. See [Training Settings](#training-settings) below. |

On a phone, the tabs are one row that you swipe sideways.

## Getting Started

1. **Check your exercise library** on the **Exercises** tab. Every gym starts with a set of default exercises, and you can add your own.
2. **Build a program** on the **Programs** tab: its weeks, workout days, exercises and sets.
3. **Flag your clients** on the **Clients** tab.
4. **Assign the program.** Members get an email, and a **Training** link appears in their member portal.
5. **Follow up** on **Recent workouts**, **Needs attention** and **Flags**, and open a client's page to see their logs and progress.

Optional: if you plan weights from each athlete's training max, set up **Lifts** and save each client's maxes. See [Training Maxes and Weight Ranges](training-maxes.md).

## Training Settings

Click the **Settings** tab. Every coach can see it, but **only the gym's owner or a manager can change it**.

| Card | What it controls | Where it's explained |
|---|---|---|
| **Weight ranges** | How planned weights are worked out from a training max | [Training Maxes and Weight Ranges](training-maxes.md#weight-range-settings) |
| **Readiness check-in** | Whether members rate how they feel before a workout | Below |
| **Needs attention** | Which clients show on the Needs attention tab | [Recent Workouts, Comments and Needs Attention](follow-up.md#needs-attention-settings) |
| **Flag rules** | Which saved sets raise a flag on the Flags tab | [Performance Flags and Flag Rules](performance-flags.md#flag-rules) |
| **Lifts** | The lifts that program rows and training maxes are based on | [Training Maxes and Weight Ranges](training-maxes.md#lifts) |

### Readiness check-in

Before a workout, members can rate how ready they feel, from 1 to 5, where **5 is always best**. It's optional for members, never blocks logging, and **never changes planned weights**.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Ask members for a readiness check-in** | Turns the check-in on or off for your gym | On |
| **Questions** | Which questions are asked: Sleep, Energy, Stress, Soreness, Mood, Fatigue. Tick at least one. | Sleep, Energy, Stress, Soreness |
| **Low readiness at or below** | 1.5, 2.0, 2.5 or 3.0. At or below this, the score shows red; from 3.5 it shows green, and amber in between. | 2.5 |

Click **Save readiness**. Turning the check-in off or unticking a question deletes nothing.

Coaches see each workout's readiness score on **Recent workouts**, on the client's **Logs** tab, and as a trend on the client's **Progress** tab. A low score can also put a client on **Needs attention**.
