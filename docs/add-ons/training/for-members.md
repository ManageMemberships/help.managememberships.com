# For Members: My Training and My Progress

If your gym coaches you with Training, your coach assigns you a program and you log your workouts from your member portal. This page explains how.

:::note Beta
Training is marked **Beta** in the app, so things may change.
:::

---

## Finding Your Program

When your coach assigns you a program, you get an email called **Your new program: …** with an **Open My Training** button. A **Training** link also appears in your member portal menu.

If your program hasn't started yet, you see its first week and the date it starts. You can start logging on the start date.

The page works on a phone and on a computer.

---

## Choosing the Week and Day

- Use the arrows to change the **week**. You can open any week up to the current one. The next week opens on its Monday (for example, *Week 4 opens Mon 9 Nov*), unless your coach opens it early.
- Tap a **day** to open that workout. Today's workout is marked **Today**. A finished day shows **Done**, and a partly logged day shows how many exercises are done, like *2 of 5*.

---

## Readiness Check-in

If your gym uses it, a **Readiness** card asks how you feel before the workout, for example your sleep, energy, stress and soreness. Tap a number from 1 to 5 for each question. **5 is always best**. To clear an answer, tap the same number again.

The check-in is optional. You can change your answers until the end of the day.

---

## Logging Your Sets

Each exercise card shows:

- the **Planned** sets, for example *3 × 8 @ 135 lb* or a weight range like *300–305 lb*, plus the target RPE and rest
- **Last time:** what you did in the same workout last week
- your coach's notes
- a play button to **Watch demo**, if there's a video

To log an exercise:

1. For each set, enter the **Weight** (optional) and **Reps**. You can also enter the **RPE**: how hard the set felt, where 10 means you had no reps left.
2. Use **Add set** or **✕** to change the number of sets.
3. Tap **Save**. After saving, the button reads **Update**.

Tap **lb** or **kg** to choose your unit. It changes the label, not the numbers you typed, and it's remembered.

When you beat your best, you see **New PR!**

If exercises are grouped as **Superset A**, do them back to back: one set of each, then the next set. You still log each exercise on its own card.

| Field | Allowed |
|---|---|
| Sets | 1 to 20 |
| Weight | 0 to 2,000 lb (907 kg), or empty |
| Reps | 1 to 100 |
| RPE | 1 to 10, in half steps |

If you missed a workout, you can log it later by going back to its week.

---

## How Did It Feel?

When every exercise of the day is logged, you can choose how the workout felt: **Very easy**, **Easy**, **Moderate**, **Hard** or **Very hard**. This is optional, and your coach sees it. Tap **Clear** to remove it.

---

## Notes and Comments

- **Workout note:** tell your coach how the workout went, then tap **Save note**. Your coach can see it.
- **Comments with your coach:** your coach's comments and thumbs-up show here. To reply, write in **Write a comment…** and tap **Post**. You can **Edit** or **Delete** your own comments.

You get an email when your coach comments. On a phone, the note and the comments share one card, with **Note** and **Comments** tabs.

---

## Body Weight

On the **Body weight** card, enter your **Weight today** and tap **Save**. If you save again on the same day, it updates that day's weight. It uses your lb or kg unit.

---

## My Progress

Tap **My progress** at the top of the page. It has these tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Exercises** | Every exercise you've logged, with your best set and PRs. Tap one to see a chart and its history. Your **Training maxes** from your coach are shown here too. |
| **History** | Your workouts by date |
| **Body weight** | A chart and every weigh-in. You can **Edit** or **Delete** the ones you added. |
| **Readiness** | Your check-in scores over time, if your gym uses check-ins |
