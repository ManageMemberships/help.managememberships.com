# Training

**Training** is an add-on for gyms that coach members. A coach builds a **program** of workout days and exercises, picks the members who are their **clients**, and assigns each client a program. Members then log what they actually did from their member portal, and their coach can see it.

Training is **off by default**. An owner turns it on from the [Marketplace](../settings/marketplace.md).

---

## Turning It On

1. Go to **Settings → Marketplace**.
2. On the **Training** card, click **Enable**.
3. Click **Turn on** in the confirmation box.

An **Add-ons** menu now appears after **Calendar**, with a **Training** link.

---

## Who Can Coach

Coaches are:

- **Owners**
- **Managers**
- **Staff** who have the **Trainer or Available for Classes** checkbox ticked on the [Staff](../settings/staff.md) page

Only coaches see **Training** in the **Add-ons** menu. Other staff and accountants don't see it and can't open its pages.

:::note
The **Trainer or Available for Classes** checkbox isn't shown on Free and Basic (Starter) plans. On those plans, only owners and managers can coach.
:::

## Programs

Go to **Add-ons → Training**. The **Programs** tab lists your gym's programs.

A program has a **name**, an optional **description**, and a **length of 4 to 6 weeks**.

### Creating a program

1. Click **New program**.
2. Enter a **Name** and, if you like, a **Description**.
3. Set **Length in weeks**, from 4 to 6.
4. Click **Create**. The program opens so you can add its workout days.

### Adding workout days and exercises

Click **Days & exercises** on a program's row. You land here automatically after creating a program.

1. Click **Add workout day**. Choose the weekday and, if you like, a title such as *Lower body*. A program can have one workout day for each weekday.
2. Under the day, click **Add exercise**. Enter the exercise **name**, the **sets** and **reps**, and optional **notes**.
3. Add as many days and exercises as you need.
4. Click **Save program**.

The same weekly plan repeats every week of the program.

### Editing and deleting a program

Click **Edit** on a program's row to change its name, description or length, then **Save changes**. Click **Delete** to remove it.

A program that a member is currently on can't be deleted. Assign that member another program first.

---

## Clients

Click the **Clients** tab. It lists your clients with each one's **Current program** and **Current week**.

- A **trainer** sees only their own clients.
- **Owners and managers** see everyone's clients, with a **Coach** column.

### Flagging clients

1. Click **Flag clients**.
2. Owners and managers choose the **Coach**. A trainer flags clients for themselves, so they aren't asked.
3. Search for and pick one or more **Members**. Only active members can be picked.
4. Click **Flag clients**.

### Unflagging a client

Click **Unflag** on the client's row and confirm. The member stops being that coach's client. If that was the member's last coach, their current program ends too.

---

## Assigning a Program

1. On the client's row, click **Assign program**.
2. Choose the **Program** and the **Start week**.
3. Click **Assign**.

The program starts on the **Monday** of the week you pick. For example, picking any day from Mon 2 Nov to Sun 8 Nov starts the program on Mon 2 Nov.

A member has **one current program** at a time. If the client already has one, the box shows it and you need to tick **End the current program and start this one** before you can assign. The old program ends and its history is kept.

The **Current program** and **Current week** columns show where each client is, for example **Week 1 of 4**, or **Starts Mon 2 Nov** if it hasn't started yet.

---

## Viewing Logs

Click **View logs** on a client's row to see their current program. For each week and workout day, it lists every exercise with the planned **sets × reps** next to what the member logged, or **Not logged**. Later weeks show **Upcoming**.

A trainer can open logs only for their own clients.

---

## For Members: the Training Page

When a member has a current program, a **Training** link appears in your member portal menu. It opens their Training page, at `/my-training` on your portal. Members who don't have a program don't see the link.

On the page, a member:

1. Chooses the **week** with the arrows. They can go from week 1 up to the current week, but not into the future.
2. Taps a **day** chip to choose the workout day.
3. For each exercise, enters the **weight** (optional, in lb), the **sets** and the **reps** they did.
4. Taps **Save**. Once an exercise has been logged, the button reads **Update**.

Saving again changes the same entry. A member who missed a session can log it late by choosing an earlier week.

The page is designed for phones.

| Field | Allowed |
|---|---|
| Weight | 0 to 2,000 lb, or empty |
| Sets | 1 to 20 |
| Reps | 1 to 100 |

---

## Good to Know

- Each exercise has **one entry per week**: one weight, one number of sets and one number of reps. There is no per-set detail.
- **Any coach can edit any program** in your gym. Editing a program also changes it for other coaches' clients who are on it.
