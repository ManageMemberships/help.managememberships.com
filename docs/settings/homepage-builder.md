# Homepage Builder

The **Homepage Builder** lets you design a fully custom public homepage for your portal with a drag-and-drop editor — no coding required. Visitors who aren't logged in will see your custom page; logged-in members continue to see the standard member homepage.

---

## Opening the Builder

1. Go to **Settings → Portal Settings**
2. Expand the **Homepage Builder** section
3. Click **Open Homepage Builder**

:::note
Access requires the **Manage Settings** permission.
:::

---

## Starter Templates

Don't want to start from a blank page? Choose one of four professionally designed starter templates and customize from there:

- **Classic** — Confident & athletic. Navy/blue color scheme with stats and grid pricing.
- **Bold** — Premium & dramatic. Near-black with gold accents and list pricing.
- **Warm** — Friendly & approachable. Indigo/coral with compact pricing.
- **Minimal** — Editorial & refined. Off-white, photo-free, with grid pricing.

Every template pulls in your real membership levels automatically, so the preview matches what visitors will actually see.

---

## Building Blocks

Drag blocks from the panel onto the canvas to build your page:

- **Headers** — several styles including logo + links, dark bar with button, and logo + tagline
- **Welcome Hero** — a large banner section for your headline and call-to-action
- **Layout columns** — 1, 2, 3, or 4 column sections for arranging content
- **Testimonials** — classic card, quote-first, side-by-side, and dark highlight styles
- **Footers** — simple centered, bold dark band, split left-right, and bordered card styles
- **Membership Levels widget** — see below
- **Google Reviews widget** — see below

You can also upload your own images directly in the builder.

## Membership Levels Widget

The **Membership Levels** widget displays your active membership plans with live pricing, descriptions, signup fees, and sign-up buttons. Click the **⚙ Design** button on the widget to customize:

### Layout
- **Grid (cards)** — side-by-side pricing cards
- **Stacked List** — full-width rows
- **Compact Row** — condensed horizontal layout

### Styling
- Card background, border color, border radius, and shadow (none / subtle / strong)
- Plan name, price, and description text colors
- Button background, text color, and border radius
- Column gap spacing

You can also choose which membership levels appear in the widget and edit the section heading text.

### Coupon Code

Want your **Join Now** buttons to carry a discount code? Choose a code on the widget and every button links to the registration page with the code already filled in, so the visitor sees it applied. You don't need **Edit HTML** for this.

1. Go to **Settings → Portal Settings → Homepage Builder** and click **Open Homepage Builder**
2. Click a **Membership Levels** widget, then press the **⚙ Design** button
3. Find the **Coupon code** dropdown and choose a code
4. Click **Done**, then **Save**

The dropdown lists **None** plus your discount codes that can currently be used. Codes are created under **Membership → Discount Codes** — see [Discount Codes](../membership/discount-codes.md).

- **One code per widget** — the code applies to the whole widget, not to individual cards. If you have two Membership Levels widgets on the page, each can use a different code.
- **Codes limited to certain levels** — if a code is limited under **Membership Restrictions → Allowed Membership Levels**, only the **Join Now** buttons for those levels carry it.
- **Codes that stop working** — if a code expires, is turned off, or reaches its usage limit, the **Join Now** buttons go back to normal links on their own. The dropdown shows the saved code with "(inactive)" after it, for example **SUMMER25 (inactive)**, so you can see why.
- **Only these buttons** — this affects the **Join Now** buttons inside the Membership Levels widget only. Other buttons on the page, such as a separate button block lower down, are not part of it. Their links are set in that button's own link settings.

:::note
Picking or changing a code needs the **Manage Membership Levels** permission. Staff who can open the Homepage Builder without it see the dropdown disabled with the saved code shown, and a saved code stays in place when they save the page.
:::

## Google Reviews Widget

The **Google Reviews** widget (found in the **Reviews** block category) displays real reviews from your Google Business Profile right on your homepage — star rating, review count, and up to 5 Google reviews (shown in Google's relevance order) with reviewer names and photos.

### Connecting Your Business

1. Drag the **Google Reviews** block onto the canvas (the connect dialog opens automatically), or click **⚙ Design** on an existing widget
2. Paste your Google Business Profile link, **or** type your business name to search
3. Click **This is my business** on the correct match

The widget fills in with your live reviews immediately.

### What Visitors See

- Your overall star rating and total Google review count
- Up to 5 Google reviews (Google provides at most 5, in its own relevance order)
- A **Leave a Review** button that takes visitors straight to Google's review form
- A **View More Reviews** button linking to your Google listing

### Keeping Reviews Fresh

Reviews are cached and **refreshed automatically every night** — you don't need to do anything to keep them up to date. If your rating or reviews change on Google, your homepage catches up within a day.

---

## Publishing

Click **Save** in the builder to publish your homepage immediately. Your custom page replaces the default homepage for logged-out visitors right away.

To go back to the default homepage, remove the custom content and save.

---

## Who Sees What

| Visitor | Homepage shown |
|---|---|
| Not logged in | Your custom builder homepage |
| Logged-in member | The standard member homepage |

This keeps marketing content (signup calls-to-action, pricing pitches) in front of prospects while members land on the familiar portal view.
