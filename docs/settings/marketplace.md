# Marketplace

The **Marketplace** is where you turn optional features, called **add-ons**, on or off for your gym. Nothing changes for your gym until you turn an add-on on, and each gym chooses for itself.

---

## Opening the Marketplace

Go to **Settings → Marketplace**.

Only **owners** and **managers** can use it. Staff and accountants don't see the link and can't open the page.

---

## Turning an Add-on On or Off

Each add-on is a card showing its **name**, a short **description**, its **version**, and an **On** or **Off** badge.

1. Click **Enable** on an add-on that is Off, or **Disable** on one that is On.
2. A confirmation box asks you to confirm, for example **Turn Training on for your gym?** Click **Turn on** (or **Turn off**), or click **Cancel** to leave it as it is.
3. The page reloads and shows a message such as *Training is now on.*

Changes apply to everyone at your gym.

---

## The Add-ons Menu

When at least one add-on is on, an **Add-ons** menu appears in the owner menu, after **Calendar**. It holds the links for the add-ons you have turned on, and each person only sees the links they are allowed to use.

If every add-on is off, the **Add-ons** menu isn't shown.

---

## Turning an Add-on Off

Turning an add-on off hides its pages for everyone at your gym. **Nothing is deleted.** The add-on's data is kept, and it comes back when you turn the add-on on again.

---
