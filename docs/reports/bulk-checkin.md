# Bulk Check-In

The **Bulk Check-In** page is the fastest way to check a whole class in at once — pick the class, date, and time, then just type names. It's ideal for taking attendance after a class ends, catching up on a missed day, or working the front desk during a rush.

Find it under **Reports → Bookings → Bulk Check-In**.

---

## How It Works

1. **Select a Class**
   - Choose the class from the dropdown. Only active classes are listed, and each one shows its schedule — e.g. "All Levels Jiu-Jitsu — Mon/Wed 6:00 PM" — so classes with the same name are easy to tell apart.

2. **Pick the Date**
   - Defaults to today. You can pick a past date to record attendance retroactively — those check-ins are stamped at the class time, so they show up on the correct day in the Check-In Report and Daily Roster.

3. **Pick the Time Slot**
   - If the class runs more than once that day, choose the slot. When there's only one, it's selected automatically.

4. **Type Names**
   - Start typing a member's name (or email) in the search box. Press **Enter** to check in the top match, or click any match in the list.
   - **Bundle members** are listed individually under their account holder, so you can check in exactly the person who's on the mat — searching a bundle member's name (e.g. a kid on a family plan) finds them too, and Enter checks *them* in, not the parent.

---

## Booked vs. Walk-Ins

Once a class and date are selected, the page shows two lists:

| List | Who's in it |
|------|-------------|
| **Booked** | Everyone who signed up in advance — the header shows how many have arrived (e.g. "3/8 checked in"). Each row has a one-click **Check In** button; checked-in members get a green check. If a booking was made for a specific attendee (like a child on a parent's account), the attendee's name is shown and the check-in is recorded under that name. |
| **Walk-Ins** | Members you checked in who had no signup for this class, marked with an amber "Walk-in" badge and their check-in time. |

Checking in a **booked** member links the check-in to their booking and marks them **Attended** — the same as marking attendance in Class Registrations. Checking in anyone else records a **walk-in** check-in for the class, just like the kiosk does.

---

## Undo

Every check-in made from this page has an **Undo** link. Undoing removes the check-in, restores any check-in quota it consumed, and clears the booking's attended status if it was set.

---

## Tips

- Check-in quotas are enforced: if a member is out of check-ins, you'll see an error instead of a silent overage.
- A member can only be checked in once per class per day from this page — a second attempt shows "already checked in."
- Walk-ins recorded here appear on the [Daily Roster](/docs/daily-roster), the [Class Calendar](/docs/reports/class-calendar), and the [Check-in Report](/docs/reports/check-in-report) automatically.
